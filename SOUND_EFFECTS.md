# Sound effects starter library

> **Downloaded (step 29b, after review):**
> - **In the app:** 58 of the BigSoundBank sounds below are bundled in `public/sounds/` (7.5 MB, OGG), as `bsb-<number>.ogg`, and listed in `src/core/sound/recorded.ts`.
> - **Blocked:** the site started refusing automated downloads part-way, so 34 weren't fetched. They can be downloaded by hand from their pages and added to `recorded.ts`.
> - **Left out:** 7 longer than 25 s (background recordings).
> - **Not downloaded:** the Freesound sounds (they need a login) and the Kenney packs (zip files, names unchecked).

A shortlist of 137 short sound effects for the "sound" object that kids drop on the bottom track of the timeline, and that gets mixed into exported videos. Every entry below is CC0 (public domain), so the files can be bundled in the app and used in kids' exports with no attribution and no per-video credit. Most come from BigSoundBank (99), then Freesound (27, CC0 filter) and Kenney packs (11). The list was assembled in November 2026 from web pages only; no file was downloaded or listened to.

## Notes

- **Counts:** 137 listed, all CC0 (99 BigSoundBank, 27 Freesound, 11 Kenney). Pick the best 100 after listening, or ship all; they are small.
- **Verification level.** BigSoundBank: each URL came from the site's own category or search listing (title, path, duration read from there); three sound pages were opened in full (Boing cartoon #3, Planted cartoon #1, Drum roll 1 L) and confirmed CC0 with OGG about 50 KB. Freesound: found with the "Creative Commons 0" filter on the search page; individual sound pages were not opened (except one non-CC0 check that was rejected), so confirm the license badge on each. Freesound durations were not reliably readable, shown as "n/a". Kenney: pack pages confirm CC0, but individual file names (for example `impactWood_medium_000.ogg`, `click_001.ogg`, `laserSmall_000.ogg`, `forceField_000.ogg`) are from memory and not verified.
- **Length column.** BigSoundBank lengths come from the listing (rounded seconds). "(trim)" means the source file is much longer than a sound effect needs and should be cut to about 1 to 3 s (or kept as a loop for ambiences).
- **Gaps.** No good CC0 result was reached for: airplane, pure rain, wind, body falls (a person tumbling), and a plain whistle blow. BigSoundBank has Aircraft, Rain, Wind, Whistles, Fight and Glass categories that were not mined; Freesound CC0 searches for "airplane", "wind", "rain", "boing" variants will fill these.
- **Content that kids might find odd.** Burp, toilet flush, snoring and kiss are kept mild but silly; drop them if the product wants a stricter tone. The "Explosion" entries are real recordings, quite loud; consider quieter cartoon alternatives (pop, cork) as defaults.
- **Rejected sources.** BBC Sound Effects (RemArc: personal, educational or research use only, not for bundling). Freesound sounds under CC BY or CC BY-NC (for example "Boing.wav" by juskiddink is CC BY 4.0, and "Comedic Boing, A" by InspectorJ appears to be attribution-licensed). Zapsplat, Samplefocus, ElevenLabs, Uppbeat, Motion Array (their own licenses, mostly attribution or no redistribution of raw files). Pixabay and Mixkit for the redistribution reasons above. OpenGameArt pages could not be fetched (HTTP 403); the CC0 packs there (for example the "63 Digital Sound Effects" pack) remain an option to check by hand.
- **Format and size.** Convert everything to OGG (Vorbis, mono, 44.1 kHz, quality about 3) with an MP3 fallback for Safari if needed; target under 200 KB per file and under 3 s except ambiences (thunder rolling, rain, sea, fire, stream, which may be looped or capped at about 10 s). Normalise loudness to about -16 LUFS so exports do not clip. Because the sounds are mixed into exported video, keep a single decoded WAV/PCM copy in memory or decode with Web Audio at export time.
- **Credits.** None are required. Optionally add a line in an About page such as "Sounds from BigSoundBank, Freesound contributors and Kenney (all CC0)".
- **Legal double-check.** CC0 is a waiver, but a few Freesound uploaders may not own what they upload; for long-lived products keep this file as the record of source URL and license per sound, and re-open each page once to confirm the license badge.
