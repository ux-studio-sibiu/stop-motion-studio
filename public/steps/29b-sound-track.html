Build steps · Step 29b · Part 10 Kids, polish and shipping

Sound track

Animations can have sound effects. A new Sounds tab lists them; one press adds a sound to a new bottom row of the timeline, under the objects in time. It plays when playback reaches it, and it's in the video.

  • The Sounds tab: 76 sounds in 13 groups (Cartoon, Whooshes, Bangs, Magic, Animals, People, Footsteps, Vehicles, Machines, Home, Nature, Water, Music): 18 made by the synthesiser, and 58 recordings. Each is a small pill, so many fit. A click plays it. Dragging it onto the stage adds it at the selected picture (or the start); dragging it onto the Sound Layer adds it where it's dropped. Either way it's picked and played, and a hidden Sound Layer shows as soon as the drag starts.
  • Timeline layers: a stacked-layers button with a caret, first in the timeline's buttons, opens a list below it, over the timeline. Its two switches show or hide the Objects Layer and the Sound Layer. Both show at first, and the choice is remembered (timelineLayers in remembered.ts). A hidden row still plays and still goes in the video, and adding a sound shows the Sounds row again.
  • The sound row: each sound is a bar as long as it sounds. Drag it to move it in time, or use the arrow keys; click it to pick it and hear it. Overlapping sounds go in rows of their own.
  • The picked sound, at the top of the tab: Quiet, Normal or Loud, and Delete sound. Adding, moving, volume and deleting are one undo step each.
  • Anchored to time, like objects in time: moving or deleting pictures doesn't move a sound. It's saved with the animation and in backups.
  • Save as video mixes the sounds into an audio track: AAC in an MP4, Opus in a WebM. The GIF stays silent.

What was built

  • The data, src/core/model/sound.ts: SoundClip (id, start in ticks, sound, volume) on Project.sounds. With soundsOf, soundTicks, moveSoundClip, soundLanes and soundsFrom, which works out what to play from a given tick. Plain code, tested in Node.
  • The library, src/core/sound/library.ts: each sound is a few notes for the synthesiser. Notes can now glide (a boing) or be noise (a whoosh, a drum roll).
  • The command: setSounds, one command for the whole list, like setOverlays. Backups (core/archive) carry the sounds, checked and capped at 500.
  • Sound in the browser, src/adapters/sound/sound.ts:
    • soundBuffer renders a sound once and keeps it;
    • previewSound plays one now, playSounds plays the row from a point;
    • mixSounds mixes the row for a video.
  • Video, videoExport.ts: an AudioBufferSource next to the video track, where the browser can encode it.
  • On screen:
    • src/studio/timeline/SoundTrack.tsx: the row;
    • src/studio/soundEditing.ts: adding, changing, deleting, and where a new sound goes;
    • src/features/sounds/SoundsPanel.tsx: the tab;
    • pickedSound in the editor store;
    • usePlayback plays the sounds along.

Decisions

  • Recorded sounds added after review: 58 CC0 recordings from BigSoundBank, in public/sounds/ (7.5 MB) and listed in src/core/sound/recorded.ts, with the length of each file. They're fetched and decoded the first time they're used (soundBuffer). The site blocked further automated downloads, so 34 more from the list are still to fetch by hand; 7 long background recordings were left out.
  • Made by the synthesiser at first: nothing is downloaded or licensed yet. The 137 recorded CC0 candidates in SOUND_EFFECTS.md need downloading and a listen first. They'll join as library entries with a file; a project only stores the sound's id, so nothing else changes.
  • Dragged to add, clicked to hear (changed after review): a sound goes where it's put, on the stage or on the Sound Layer, like objects. A click only plays it, so a child can try sounds without adding them. Two sounds dropped quickly both stay: each change builds on the last one saved (soundEditing.ts).
  • Start in ticks, length in seconds: a speed change keeps a sound on the same picture, and it still sounds as long.
  • Played on the audio clock: every sound is scheduled when Play is pressed, again when a loop comes round. Timers would drift from the pictures.
  • Its own pick (pickedSound), beside the timeline's selection of pictures or one object bar, so picking a sound never changes what the stage shows.

Watch out

  • Sounds after the last picture are kept on the row but aren't played or put in the video, like objects in time.
  • The toolbar's Duplicate and Delete act on pictures and object bars, not sounds: a sound is deleted from the Sounds tab or with the Delete key on its bar.
  • A browser that can't encode audio makes the video without sound, silently. Playwright's Chromium can (checked); Safari and Firefox weren't tried.
  • A sound from a newer studio is kept and shown as "Sound", one second long, and stays silent.

Check it

  • Select picture 3, open Sounds, press Boing: it plays and sits under picture 3. Make it Loud, play: it's louder.
  • Add Ding at the start, drag Boing later, play: both sound at their pictures. Change the speed: they stay with their pictures.
  • Save as video and open it in the computer's player: the sounds are there, in time.
  • Undo a few times: the sounds go back step by step.
