Help

A Help button (a question mark) at the right of the studio's top bar opens a window with how to make an animation and every keyboard shortcut the studio has. The ? key opens it too. The roadmap's step 30 (accessibility scans, a keyboard-only test run, screenshot tests) was replaced by this on request.

  • Making an animation: six short steps, from taking a picture to saving a video.
  • The keys, by where they work: anywhere, the Camera tab, the timeline, the Objects and Sound layers, the Picture and Objects tabs on the stage, and the side panel. Each shows its keys as key caps, with what they do.
  • Escape, the cross, or a click outside closes it. While it's open, the keyboard stays inside it.

Also in this change: clicking a sound in the Sounds tab plays at most 5 seconds of it, fading out (the music is 30 s long). Clicking another sound stops the one playing.

Since then: the Help button sits in the column beside the stage, under Save (D5).

What was built

  • src/studio/HelpButton.tsx: the button, the ? key, and the window. It's a modal <dialog>, with its contents written out directly in JSX.
  • src/studio/styles/_help.scss: the window, with only its contents scrolling, and the key caps. On a narrow screen the keys go above what they do.
  • src/adapters/sound/sound.ts: previewSound plays at most PREVIEW_SECONDS (5) and stops the previous preview. A sound still loading when another is clicked isn't played after all.

Decisions

  • The list is written by hand, not read from the code. The shortcuts live in a dozen places (the timeline, the stage, the history), each with its own rules. A shortcut changed in the code has to be changed in HelpButton.tsx too; its comment says so.
  • Ctrl everywhere, with a note that a Mac uses Cmd.

Check it

  • Press ?: the window opens. Escape closes it. Do the same with the button.
  • Click Ukulele in the Sounds tab: about 5 seconds, then it fades. Click it again and then Meow: the tune stops at once.
Checked while building: lint and typecheck are clean; the stage, sounds and camera specs pass (26), against the dev server. Not looked at by eye.
