Build steps · Step 24b (added) · Part 8 Elements

Free drawing on pictures

A new Draw tab: draw on the picture on the stage (camera off) with a pencil, in a colour and one of four widths, and rub lines out with the eraser.

  • Lines are smoothed: when you let go, the hand's jitter is taken out and points the line doesn't need are dropped. It's drawn as smooth curves with round ends.
  • The eraser takes away every line it touches. A ring shows where it is.
  • Clear drawing takes the whole drawing off the picture.
  • Each line, each erase and Clear drawing is one undo step.
  • The tool, the colour and the width are remembered after a reload. Picking a colour or a width takes the pencil again.
  • A picture's drawing is one object: the thumbnail shows it as it's drawn, see-through shows the previous picture's, playback and export include it, and copying a picture copies it. In the Objects tab it can be moved, sized, turned or deleted as one.
Added after the step: copy and paste between pictures.
  • Ctrl+C copies the picked object in the Objects tab (on the picture or the time track), or the picture's drawing in the Draw tab. Ctrl+V pastes it onto every selected picture, or the one on the stage, in the same place, so something carried to the next picture doesn't need lining up again. One undo step per paste, however many pictures; the copy on the stage is picked.
  • A drawing pasted onto a picture that has one joins it (one drawing per picture). An object from the time track is pasted onto the picture as an ordinary object; bars have Duplicate for the track.
  • The copy is kept inside the app: across pictures and animations, not after a reload. Text fields keep their own copy and paste.
  • Built: copyOf and pasteInto (core/model/clipboard.ts), mergeDrawings, the setElementsOf command (several pictures' objects in one step; setElements is now the one-picture case of it), the store's clipboard, and useObjectClipboard (the keys) in src/studio/.
  • Tests: 4 unit tests for copy and paste, 2 for setElementsOf against storage, 1 for the store's clipboard; end-to-end, pasting a bubble onto one picture and then three (one undo), and a drawing joining another.

What was built

  • The model, src/core/model/drawing.ts:
    • the drawing kind (DRAWING in objectKinds.ts): strokes with their points in the drawing's own box, a colour and a width (PENCIL_WIDTHS, a share of the frame's height, so lines look the same at any size);
    • simplifyStroke: a light average, then Ramer-Douglas-Peucker, ends kept;
    • addStroke: the first stroke makes the drawing; later ones are added and the box is fitted around them all (while it isn't turned);
    • eraseStrokes: the strokes within reach of the eraser's path go; none left, the drawing goes;
    • drawingProps (read defensively) and strokePoints.
  • Drawing: src/core/render/drawing.ts, drawDrawing and drawStroke (curves through the midpoints, round ends; a single point is a dot). elements.ts gains one line.
  • The Draw tab, src/features/draw/: DrawPanel, DrawLayer (the stage: the line as it's drawn on a canvas of its own, then smoothed and saved), drawSettings (the remembered tool, colour and width, the picture drawn on), _draw.scss. Studio.tsx places the tab and its stage layer directly.
  • Shared, moved to src/studio/ so the Draw and Objects tabs both use them without depending on each other: pictureObjects.ts (showing and saving a picture's objects; overlayEditing.ts does the same for the track), Swatches.tsx (the colour rows and palettes), and the field and swatch styles (_side-panel.scss).
  • The Objects tab knows drawings: a pencil icon, and a note to edit it in the Draw tab.
  • remembered.ts: drawTool, drawColour, drawWidth.

Decisions

  • One drawing per picture, all its strokes in one object, not one object per stroke, so picking things in the Objects tab isn't cluttered with dozens of lines.
  • Strokes in the drawing's own box, like a bubble's tail: moving or sizing the drawing takes its lines along. While the box isn't turned it's kept fitted around the lines; once turned, new lines are added in its turned terms.
  • Smoothing when the line is finished, not while it's drawn: the line follows the pointer exactly as you draw, and what's kept is light (a couple of hundred pointer points become a few dozen).
  • The eraser takes whole strokes. Simple to understand and to undo; cutting a line in two can come later.
  • Not on the time track yet. The model already allows it: an overlay's object can be a drawing. What's left is the Draw layer choosing an overlay to draw into, the way the Objects tab already picks between a picture and an overlay. See PLAN.md §3.

Watch out

  • Touch: the drawing layer stops a finger from scrolling (touch-action: none), and uses the pointer's coalesced events, so fast lines don't get corners.
  • See-through tints the stage as before, so a line can look a different colour while see-through is on.
  • Turned drawings: new lines can reach past a turned drawing's box; its handles in the Objects tab then don't wrap the whole drawing. Rare, and harmless.

Check it

  • Draw a wobbly line: it comes out smooth, and the thumbnail shows it.
  • Draw two lines, erase one, undo, Clear drawing, undo.
  • Pick blue, Huge and the eraser, reload: they're as left.
  • In the Objects tab, move the drawing; copy the picture: the copy has it too.
