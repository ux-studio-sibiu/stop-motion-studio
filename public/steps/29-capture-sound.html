Capture sound

Taking a picture now makes a short click, so a child knows it worked without looking at the screen. A Sound button in the Camera tab, above the camera switch, turns it off and on.

  • The click plays once the picture is saved, so a failed save stays silent. It's the same for the button, Space and Tab.
  • It's on at first, and the setting is remembered. Turning it on plays it once.
  • The roadmap's other ideas for this step (a timer, a celebration, new count wording) were left out on request. Sound effects in the animation itself are planned as step 29b (ROADMAP.md), with a list of candidate sounds in SOUND_EFFECTS.md.

What was built

  • The sound, src/core/sound/sounds.ts: SHUTTER, two short notes for a tiny synthesiser, and notesLength. Plain code, tested in Node.
  • Playing it, src/adapters/sound/sound.ts: playNotes and scheduleNotes, with Web Audio (oscillators with a quick fade in and out). There are no sound files.
  • When: src/studio/useCapture.ts takes a sound flag and plays the shutter after a successful save.
  • The setting: src/features/camera/CaptureSoundToggle.tsx, placed by Studio.tsx inside CameraPanel, which takes children above the switch. remembered.ts has captureSound.

Decisions

  • On by default: it's a confirmation, the reason for having it. One press turns it off for a quiet classroom.
  • Synthesised, not recorded: nothing to download or license, and a test can check the notes. The sound effects planned for 29b are real recordings, from a bundled library.
  • A UI sound, not part of the animation: it never goes into an export.

Watch out

  • Autoplay rules: browsers only allow sound after a press. The click always follows one (capture, or the Sound button), which is when the audio is set up.
  • No test hears the speakers: the browser test renders the sound offline, and the end-to-end test counts the sounds started. Check by ear below.

Check it

  • Take a picture: a click. Turn Sound off, take another: silent. Reload: still off.
  • Turn it on: one click, straight away.
Checked while building:
  • 374 unit tests and 47 browser tests pass; lint and typecheck are clean.
  • End-to-end, against the dev server: the camera spec (10) and the design spec (4). The other specs, the production build and WebKit weren't run.
  • Not heard yet: the click itself.
