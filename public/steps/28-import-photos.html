Build steps · Step 28 · Part 9 Export and import

Import photos

Add photos, a small picture-with-a-plus icon in the timeline's tools between Duplicate and Delete, adds photos from the computer, as many as wanted. Photos dropped anywhere on the studio are added too.

  • They go after the selected picture (or at the end), in name order with numbers counted as numbers (IMG_2 before IMG_10), and are selected. One undo takes them all back.
  • Each photo keeps its shape, shrunk to fit inside the animation's size (never enlarged), and turned the way the camera held it. The stage fits it in whole, as for a capture, so a portrait photo has bars at its sides.
  • While they're opened, a note above the timeline (and the button's tooltip) says how far it got. Afterwards the note says what was added and why any weren't: iPhone photos (HEIC) this browser can't open, broken files, files over 50 MB, files that aren't photos, and more than 300 at once.

What was built

  • The rules, src/core/photos/photos.ts: isPhotoFile and isHeic, photoOrder, photoSize, photoNote and PHOTO_LIMITS. Plain code, tested in Node.
  • Opening a photo, src/adapters/images/photo.ts: preparePhoto opens it with createImageBitmap(file, { imageOrientation: "from-image" }) and resizes it on an OffscreenCanvas, on white for see-through PNGs. It gives a JPEG and a thumbnail, the same Capture a camera shot gives. The thumbnail code is shared with restoring a backup (thumbnailOf in thumbnail.ts).
  • The feature, src/features/photo-import/:
    • usePhotoImport: opens the photos one at a time, then saves them all with addFrames, like a capture. The undo button's title says "Add 10 photos". Thumbnails go into memory first, and the storage check runs afterwards;
    • PhotoImportButton, placed by Studio.tsx in the timeline's tools (Timeline takes it as addPhotos), between Duplicate and Delete;
    • PhotoDrop: the dashed outline while photos are dragged over the studio, the drop, and the note.

Decisions

  • The same path as capture once the picture exists (the roadmap's plan): the same Capture and the same command, so the timeline, export and backup don't care where a picture came from.
  • Its own shape, not cut to the animation's, unlike the camera, which fills the frame. A portrait phone photo cut to 16:9 would lose most of itself. The child can still zoom it to fill the frame in the Picture tab.
  • Dropped anywhere on the studio, not only on the timeline: a bigger target for small hands. The outline covers the whole studio, so it's clear the drop will work.
  • Opened one at a time, so ten 12-megapixel photos never sit decoded in memory at once. Only the small JPEGs are kept until the save.
  • No FrameSource list (PLAN.md): placed directly, as since step 22.

Watch out

  • HEIC opens in Safari only. Elsewhere the note names those photos and says what to do; nothing is added in their place.
  • EXIF orientation is asked for explicitly, and current browsers apply it by default. No test uses a photo with an EXIF turn in it: check with a phone photo taken sideways.
  • A failed save (full storage) shows the usual message and adds nothing; the import note isn't shown then.

Check it

  • Select a picture and add 10 photos of mixed sizes, including a portrait one and a panorama. They come after it, in name order, each whole. One undo removes them all.
  • Drag a few photos from the file manager onto the studio: the outline shows, and they're added.
  • Add a phone photo taken sideways: it's upright. Add a HEIC in Chrome: the note says why it wasn't added.
